# Limitations

1. **Synthetic data until real data is available.** Every result in this repository was produced on a seeded synthetic stand-in with known ground truth. It shows that the method works and recovers known parameters. It says nothing about real Abu Dhabi accuracy. Real-data accuracy is unknown until `skystay all` is re-run with real data in `data/raw/`.
2. **Air and non-air demand are only weakly separable.** The non-air baseline (UAE residents, land arrivals, other UAE airports) is identified only from supply changes: route launches, cuts, frequency and gauge changes. On synthetic data the median error of the non-air share is about 0.10. For markets reached only through hubs, or with interrupted service, it can be much larger. Treat the per-market non-air share as indicative.
3. **Capture, nationality mix and returning residents are not separately identified.** Aggregated data cannot tell a returning resident from a visitor, or split the hotel capture rate from the nationality mix of a flight. We estimate their product: hotel guests per terminating passenger, by origin and nationality. We report that product, with its row-normalised mix, and say so.
4. **Calendar effects are under-estimated.** Events that fall on fixed dates every year cannot be told apart from ordinary month-of-year seasonality. The fitted event uplift is therefore conservative (on synthetic data it is well below the true value). The ML layer recovers part of the difference.
5. **New routes are the least certain.** Cold-start origins borrow region-average load factor, transfer share and conversion, and their parameter uncertainty is doubled. Diversion is not modelled: some travellers on a new direct route would otherwise have come via a hub. New-route scenarios may therefore overstate net new guests.
6. **Intervals rest on a short calibration set.** Conformal intervals are calibrated on 3 rolling folds, about 36 monthly residuals per market. On the synthetic holdout, the 95% interval covered 91% and the 80% interval covered 79%. Intervals for scenarios far outside the historical range are widened, but by a rule (x1.5), not by calibration.
7. **Lever effects are elasticities of the fitted model, not causal estimates.** Market capacity share was deliberately left out of the ML features to keep scenario effects local to linked markets. Competition between airports, price effects and hotel capacity limits are not modelled.
8. **Reference year.** Scenarios are applied to the latest 12 months of schedule and calendars. They are not a forecast of future demand growth.
9. **Scales are arbitrary in synthetic mode.** Synthetic magnitudes are round numbers, not calibrated to official statistics (see `data/synthetic/SOURCES.md`).
