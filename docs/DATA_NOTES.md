# Data notes

_Generated by `skystay data` from the ingested tables; coverage figures are computed, caveats are maintained in `src/skystay/io/pipeline.py`._

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA.** No real data is in `data/raw/`, so these tables come from the seeded generator in `src/skystay/io/synthetic.py` (config `data/synthetic/generator.yaml`). Its layout mimics the aggregated data a tourism planner would have (flight schedule, passenger traffic, hotel performance, calendars); its numbers say nothing about Abu Dhabi.

## The country catch

Flight data says where a plane departed; hotel data says what passport the guest holds. Hubs (e.g. London, Singapore) carry many nationalities, expatriate residents fly from their country of residence, residents return home, and some guests arrive by land or via other UAE airports. We therefore never assume the two keys are the same; we compare three reconciliation levels (identity, regional, learned bridge) in the back-test.

## Modelling window

Overlap of flights, traffic and hotel data: 2019-01 to 2025-12 (84 months). 84 overlapping months >= 36: L2 learned bridge is feasible.

See `reports/data_quality.md` for missing records, duplicates, outliers and load-factor flags.
